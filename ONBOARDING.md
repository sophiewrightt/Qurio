# Current tester behaviour

Tester sessions now skip the introduction entirely; Guest behaviour below is unchanged. `tourForProfile` starts testers at `done`, and completed tours ignore all events. Tester energy remains unlimited and no tutorial gift is granted. This supersedes earlier references to a tester tour.

# Guided introduction and local entry

`PrototypeApp` is the default entry in `App.tsx`. It first shows `PrototypeLoginScreen`: optional username, Guest / Tester, and an explicit local/session-only notice. Entering creates a fresh in-memory `App` session. Leaving via the settings sidebar unmounts it and returns to entry; reload also resets. **This is not login or authentication.** Nothing is written to Supabase, localStorage or a shared account. Tester is a public prototype mode, not an administrative authorization.

Both modes start the introduction on every entry. Tester skips the energy explanation and every lesson-energy confirmation, displays ∞, and has the isolated Creator wardrobe preview. Guest displays the updated 3/3 energy policy. Daily reset, actual energy spending and login streaks remain deferred; both entry and sidebar say so. The existing decorative streak never earns rewards.

`data/onboarding.ts` owns copy and a pure expected-step/event state machine. `components/Onboarding.tsx` provides fading hand-drawn-style bubbles, dimmed surroundings, measured spotlights, scroll-container-aware target reveal, and separate overlays inside native dictionary/packet modals. Narration says “tap anywhere to continue”; both the bubble and surrounding screen advance it, while scrolling long copy does not intentionally advance. Action steps require the real highlighted control. Anchor polling and animations clean up on step change/unmount. Session ledger rewards are not granted by overlays.

## Sequence

1. Welcome, Home, profile/sidebar, streak, energy (Guest), Quanta, empty friend area, first lesson. Before the friend message there is a one-second clear-screen pause with no highlighted area. The friend message itself has no spotlight. Unknown-avatar circle and its caption are removed. The guided Lesson 1 start is free and bypasses confirmation only once; guest replays confirm normally.
2. Three short Lesson 1 entrance messages, then free exploration. The first-quiz explanation appears when the quiz button enters the viewport, with a press fallback for fast scrolling. Knowledge is bold/gold. No interaction or quiz gate is bypassed.
3. Lesson 1 completion offers only Home. The tour highlights Knowledge then the real companion-name input. Focusing it dismisses guidance for typing; submitting or blurring a nonempty name triggers the explore-for-now message. Zero correct answers gets truthful alternative copy, not a claim of earned Knowledge. Dictionary, achievements and wardrobe remain visibly disabled until their respective guided introductions. Locked entries say “Keep playing levels to unlock!” instead of inviting entry. Once introduced, they stay available for the session.
4. Lesson 4 completion offers only Home. Its actual W1.1 achievement and viewed terminology feed the dictionary/achievement walkthrough. If no card was viewed, alternate copy explains how words are collected; no term is silently backfilled. After the dictionary explanation, the player can browse freely without an exit prompt. Closing it returns to Home and highlights Achievements; the player must tap that entry before claiming. The Quanta claim prompt has no dark shading; the successful claim gets a one-second clear-screen pause before the Home prompt. This uses the existing one-time claim ledger.
5. Return Home, open wardrobe, introduce the wardrobe, then Shop. Show the undimmed shop for one second. Packet/gift prompts then dim the screen, and the buy prompt spotlights the actual purchase button. The full-screen overlay sits outside the shop scroll view; it retains the shop scroll position rather than auto-scrolling the button to the top. Credit exactly 100 introductory Quanta once when acknowledging the gift message. It adds to existing earnings and is included in purchase affordability. After the real purchase, show the mystery packet for one second before its rip guidance. Normal fixed contents, weighted randomness, duplicate handling and inventory rules apply. Results say “Here’s what you got!” and remain freely scrollable before Back to Q-market. Explain other offers, return to wardrobe, explain drawers and finish. Action prompts never append “Tap the highlighted control”.

Pause timers run only on the matching enabled screen/modal, use expected-step guards and clean up on navigation/unmount. Feature locks are onboarding UI gates, not authentication or server authorization. The 100-Quanta gift, purchase and claim ledgers are unchanged.

New-user progression is paused between Lessons 1 and 4, not replaced by a slideshow. Exiting a lesson early does not complete it. Tour states tied to a screen remain pending until returning there. Lesson 1 and Lesson 4 Next buttons are removed, including on replays, as requested. Other Next buttons remain unchanged.
