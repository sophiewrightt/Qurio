## Flow and limits

- Save either opening electron prediction without grading it. Reveal the tennis-ball answer after either choice; then use matching electron IDs to resolve the original question.
- Supplied human and particle IDs remain side by side and open in a scrollable, dismissible enlargement with accessible transcriptions. The source electron card omits its negative charge sign; `CardArt` adds a proportional ink stroke in both sizes. Source scans remain unchanged.
- Identical means the same species and intrinsic properties, not that every quantum particle is the same species or has the same whole state. Charge is quantised; no claim that species masses form a universal evenly spaced ladder.
- The first tap on Charm's actual ID reveals the introduction directly; later taps enlarge the ID. The separate meet control is also available. Charm and strange are distinct second-generation quark species. Artwork colours and faces are illustrative, not literal quark colours or individual identifiers.
- The machine is an idealised two-mode spin illustration, not a real free-quark apparatus. Entangle first; no definite local vertical up/down is shown until measuring. An independent 50/50 sample determines one side and the other is opposite for this chosen state. Changing a state does not change species; entanglement is not necessary for different states.
- Two final spin-up charms match species and the chosen spin result only. Their spatial states may differ. Do not imply that two identical fermions occupy exactly the same complete single-particle state.
- Quark masses on the supplied IDs are illustrative approximate estimates, dependent on energy scale/convention. The enlarged cards note this; no numeric values are tested.
- Quiz replaces notes, has five explained questions, and awards the existing `20 + 5 * correct` once. No wardrobe unlock, world-finale packet, or unimplemented next lesson is added.
